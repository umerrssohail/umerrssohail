# Hi, I'm Umer Sohail 👋

**AI automation consultant · Economics Ph.D. candidate · Applied data researcher**

I build useful AI workflows and reproducible data pipelines. At TeamLift, I turn business processes into documented AI prototypes and automations. At the University of Delaware, I study how policy and institutions shape firm behavior using real-world data.

### What I work on

- **AI automation:** Design and prototype agentic workflows, tool integrations, and documented business processes using Claude, OpenAI, Codex, and Make.
- **Applied research:** Study international trade, development, and firm outcomes using administrative data and causal inference.
- **Data systems:** Build repeatable pipelines for cleaning, record linkage, geospatial analysis, and longitudinal panels.

### Current research

My job market paper studies how Pakistani manufacturing firms adjusted inputs and output after tariff reductions under the EU's GSP+ agreement. I also work on commercial courts and firm performance in India, and on the effects of barter trade policy during a foreign exchange crisis.
